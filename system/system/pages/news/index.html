✨This release

New DM system!

WannaChat is rebuilt: send friend requests, accept them, and message your friends right from the site. You'll see when a friend is typing, and a small card pops up in the corner when a new message lands. Click it to jump straight into that conversation.

🔔

DM icon shows new messages

The DM button in the header now swaps to its "new message" icon whenever a friend's latest message is unread, and goes back to normal once you've read it. Your friends' names and pictures show up in chat too.

📚

Library pages 11–15 refreshed

Assets have been re-added across pages 11 through 15 of the library, so those pages are filled back in and ready to browse, search and favorite.

🎟️

Auto approve until 300 users!

New access tickets are approved instantly, now up to the first 300 — no waiting on manual approval until that cap is reached.

📣

Update pop-up on every new version

The "Updates & changes" pop-up now appears by itself each time the site gets a new update, so you never miss what changed. Dismiss it and it stays away until the next one.
